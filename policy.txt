## Investment Policy and Compliance Guidelines

### Section 1: General Trading Principles
1.1. **Fiduciary Duty**: All investment decisions must be made in the best interest of our clients, prioritizing their financial well-being and investment objectives.
1.2. **Ethical Conduct**: All employees must adhere to the highest standards of ethical conduct, avoiding any real or perceived conflicts of interest.
1.3. **Applicable Laws**: All trading activities must comply with all applicable national and international laws, including but not limited to SEC regulations in the United States and MiFID II in the European Union.

### Section 2: Trade Size and Volume
2.1. **Maximum Position Size**: No single trade shall result in a position exceeding 10% of the fund's total assets under management (AUM).
2.2. **Daily Volume Limit**: A trade in any single security shall not exceed 5% of that security's average daily trading volume over the preceding 30 days.
2.3. **Concentration Limit**: The total value of positions in any single issuer (equity and debt combined) shall not exceed 15% of the fund's AUM.

### Section 3: Prohibited Activities
3.1. **Insider Trading**: Trading on material non-public information is strictly prohibited. All employees must report any potential exposure to such information to the compliance department immediately.
3.2. **Short Selling**: Short selling of securities is not permitted in any managed portfolio. All investment strategies must be long-only.
3.3. **Market Manipulation**: Any activity designed to manipulate market prices or trading volumes, such as wash trading or "pump and dump" schemes, is strictly forbidden.

### Section 4: Risk Management
4.1. **Liquidity Requirement**: A minimum of 20% of the total portfolio value must be maintained in cash or cash equivalents to ensure liquidity.
4.2. **Sector Exposure**: Investment in any single market sector (e.g., Technology, Healthcare) shall not exceed 25% of the total portfolio value.
4.3. **No Leverage**: The use of leverage or margin borrowing for investment purposes is strictly prohibited. All positions must be fully funded.

### Section 5: Restricted Instruments
5.1. **Complex Derivatives**: Trading in complex or exotic derivatives, including but not limited to credit default swaps (CDS) and collateralized debt obligations (CDOs), is prohibited.
5.2. **Penny Stocks**: Investment in stocks trading for less than $5.00 per share ("penny stocks") is not permitted due to their high volatility and risk.
5.3. **Non-Rated Bonds**: The fund shall not invest in non-rated or "junk" bonds (i.e., bonds with a credit rating below BBB- or Baa3).
